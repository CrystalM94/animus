Every lot offered by Animus Labs is released against a documented specification and is traceable to independent analytical testing.

Verification

Each lot is analysed by an independent, third-party analytical laboratory. Identity and purity are confirmed by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) and mass spectrometry (MS). Typical release specification is ≥98% purity; the certified result for the lot you receive is stated on its certificate of analysis.

Documentation

Every lot ships with:

  • a lot-specific certificate of analysis (COA) showing the analytical result;
  • a safety data sheet (SDS) describing safe handling, storage, and disposal;
  • a printed lot number that resolves on this site's public verification page.

Scans of current lot documents are linked from each product page. The lot printed on your label can be entered on the verification page to retrieve the same record.

Traceability

The lot supplied with each order line is recorded permanently on the order. Historical orders always resolve to the lot that actually shipped, even after newer batches enter inventory.

Handling and storage

Lyophilised materials are supplied sealed and are intended to be stored frozen and protected from light and moisture, as stated in each lot's documentation. Stability and retest dates are published per lot.

Specification guarantee

If independent verification demonstrates that a lot does not meet its stated specification, we will replace the material or refund the order in full. See the Refund Policy for the claims process.
