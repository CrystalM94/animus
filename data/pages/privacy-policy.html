This Privacy Policy describes how Animus Labs collects, uses, and protects information when you use this site.

Information we collect

  • Account and order information: name, email, billing and shipping addresses, order history, and the research-use acknowledgement recorded at checkout (including its timestamp and policy version).
  • Payment information: processed directly by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser details, and pages visited, for security, fraud prevention, and site analytics.

How we use it

  • to fulfil orders, assign shipping lots, and provide order history;
  • to comply with legal and regulatory obligations;
  • to verify lot documentation and provide customer support;
  • to detect and prevent fraud and abuse.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for cart, account, and site functionality, and a stored preference recording that you have passed the restricted-access gate. You may accept or decline non-essential cookies on first visit.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate: with our payment processor, carriers for delivery, and service providers under contract. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and rights

Order records, including lot assignments and compliance acknowledgements, are retained as required for legal, accounting, and traceability purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information subject to those obligations by contacting us.
